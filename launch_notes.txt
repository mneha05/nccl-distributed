Notes for NCCL distributed training

Use PyTorch's `torch.distributed.launch` or `torchrun` with `--nproc_per_node` and ensure NCCL backend is used:

Example:
python -m torch.distributed.run --nproc_per_node=2 train.py

Ensure `NCCL_SOCKET_IFNAME` and network settings are correct when using multiple machines.
